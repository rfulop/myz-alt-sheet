# Portrait de Nox

Fichier : `assets/images/nox-portrait.png`.

Portrait original généré le 19 octobre 2026 avec l’outil intégré OpenAI ImageGen, mode édition du portrait fourni, pour le personnage fictif de démonstration. Le visage a été redessiné à la demande de l’auteur : orientation davantage de face, traces de survie et traits de la mutation Reptilien, en conservant le foulard olive et la veste rapiécée. Il ne provient pas d’un livre Mutant Year Zero. Le JSON d’exemple et les captures du README utilisent ce même fichier.

Prompt final utilisé :

This exact attached woman is the identity reference, NOT the later goblin-like portrait. Turn her head and shoulders toward the viewer: almost frontal view, both eyes and both sides of the face clearly visible, gaze directed at the viewer, with only a slight natural asymmetry. Keep her existing facial identity, sun-weathered textured skin, healed scars, short ragged dark hair with sparse temple, olive scarf, patched rust-brown utility jacket, gritty matte ink-and-gouache treatment, pale paper background, square head-and-shoulders framing. Her actual game mutation is REPTILIAN: preserve and clarify the ochre reptile scales along her temple and cheek, amber eyes with visibly vertical slit pupils; subtly carry the scales down the side of her neck so the mutation feels biological and coherent. Keep her weary, alert survivor expression, natural imperfect face, no makeup or beauty retouching. Do NOT add goblin ears, tusks, horns, extra limbs, glamorous lighting, model posing, smoother skin, text, logos, borders or gore. Change primarily the head orientation; retain this reference's art and character.
